# Fantasy Races: Design Document

Status as of 2026-11-10. Target: Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric, Velocity network), forever world.
Components: a datapack (`races_datapack`) and an ETF/EMF resource pack (`fantasyraces_resourcepack`).

**Caveat for whoever continues this:** the author of this document last built and audited the pack on 2026-10-24 from the uploaded zip. The user has since edited files again in Blockbench and placed the newest versions in a shared folder, which was not visible when this was written. Treat the "Known issues" section as the ground truth of what is still broken, and re-verify everything below against the actual files.

---

## 1. Goal

Turn vanilla villagers into custom races (hobbit, elf, copper construct, fishfolk, kobold) with:
- a craftable or giveable "contract" item that converts a villager,
- per-race models/skins in the resource pack, selected by **scoreboard team** (not by renaming villagers),
- race-gated custom trades (hobbit only so far).

## 2. Race to model/skin map

| Race | Team (client-visible, used by ETF/EMF) | Tag (server-side, trade gating) | Skin/model number |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hobbit | `race_hobbit` | `race_hobbit` | 2 |
| Elf | `race_elf` | `race_elf` | 3 |
| Copper construct, legs | `race_copper_legs` | `race_copper`, `race_copper_legs` | 4 |
| Copper construct, uni-wheel | `race_copper_wheel` | `race_copper`, `race_copper_wheel` | 5 |
| Fishfolk | `race_fishfolk` | `race_fishfolk` | 6 |
| Kobold | `race_kobold` | `race_kobold` | 7 |
| Dwarf | `race_dwarf` | `race_dwarf` | 8 |

Dwarf (added 2026-11-10) is fully generated by `build_dwarf.py` (needs the 26.3 client jar): 128x128 texture with the vanilla layout top-left for vanilla-shaped parts (head, nose, hat, hat rim, arms) and remapped body/robe/legs plus a beard box in the right half. All 26 vanilla overlays are remapped automatically into `<name>8.png`, so dwarves wear vanilla profession/biome/level clothing. Legs are body submodels with a hip pivot at y=6, swung by the vanilla walk formula in the body animation (the vanilla leg parts are left empty). Preview any model offline with `preview_jem.py`. Contract: paper + gold ingot + cobbled deepslate (placeholder).

Why teams: scoreboard tags are server-only and never reach the client. Teams are synced, so ETF's `teams.N=` property can match them.
EMF `models.N=` is assumed to share ETF's property matching. It works in practice, since the kobold model displays, but I never found documentation confirming `teams` support for `models.N`.

## 3. Datapack (`races`)

Format: `pack.mcmeta` with `min_format`/`max_format` 121 (the 26.3 data pack format per community sources; unverified against the server log). Folder names are singular (`function`, `recipe`, `advancement`, `predicate`).

**Implemented**
- `minecraft:load` tag runs `races:load`, which creates the six teams.
- Contract flow, per race: `advancement/contract/<race>.json` (trigger `player_interacted_with_entity`, paper with `custom_data.race_contract`, entity inline `minecraft:entity_properties` villager) leads to the reward function `contract/apply_<race>`. That function revokes the advancement, tags the nearest villager within 6 blocks `race_pending`, clears one contract, runs `races:set/<race>`, and removes the temp tag.
- `function/set/<look>.mcfunction`: removes every race tag, adds the new tag(s), `team join race_<look> @s`, plays a sound and spawns a particle.
- Copper construct is one race with a random look rolled once (`races:set/copper`, using predicate `races:coin_flip`, `random_chance` 0.5). It clears `race_copper_legs` first so a re-roll can reach wheel. `set/copper_legs` and `set/copper_wheel` force a look.
- `set/clear` resets a villager to vanilla.
- `give/<race>_contract` functions (ops).
- Shapeless recipes for every contract, unlocked by holding paper. The hobbit recipe is paper + wheat + fern. The others are **placeholders** (elf: lily of the valley + glow berries; copper: copper ingot + redstone; fishfolk: kelp + cod; kobold: pointed dripstone + coal).
- Hobbit-only data-driven trades: farmer L1-4 and fisherman L1-2, added through `data/minecraft/tags/villager_trade/<profession>/level_N.json` (`replace:false`). Each trade has a `merchant_predicate` requiring `minecraft:entity_tags: all_of [race_hobbit]`.
- Trades only roll at job claim and level-up, so convert the villager before it takes a job.

**26.3 syntax notes (learned the hard way)**
- Predicates and loot conditions use `"type"`, not `"condition"`.
- Advancement entity fields take a single inline predicate (not a list).
- Entity predicates are component-style (`minecraft:entity_type`, `minecraft:entity_tags`).

**Open datapack items**
1. Never confirmed after the syntax fix that the pack loads cleanly and that `races:` functions show in autofill. Check `latest.log` first.
2. Wheel variant: fixed (the bug was `set/copper` never clearing the legs tag), but not confirmed in game.
3. Only hobbits have trades. Elf, copper, fishfolk and kobold need `villager_trade` files, and the recipes need real ingredients.
4. The old `build.py` in the repo (hobbit-only, uses `condition`/NBT predicate) is **obsolete**. Use `build_datapack.py`.

## 4. Resource pack (ETF + EMF)

Layout under `assets/minecraft/optifine/`:
- `random/entity/villager/villager.properties`: base skin rules (`skins.N` / `teams.N`).
- `random/entity/villager/{profession,type,profession_level}/*.properties` and matching PNGs: overlay rules.
- `cem/villager.properties`: model rules (`models.N=<n>` means `villager<n>.jem`, gated by `teams.N`).
- `cem/villager2..7.jem`: one model per look (Blockbench exports).
- Skins are `textures/entity/villager/villager<N>.png`, with copies for the random folder.

**Overlay strategy.** ETF layers profession/biome/level overlays over the base skin. Hobbit keeps its own overlays (`skins.1=2`). Fishfolk, copper-wheel and kobold have their overlays **disabled** by team-gated rules pointing at fully transparent PNGs (`<name><skin>.png`, 26 files each, generated by `build_rp.py`'s `BLANK` list). When real overlays exist for a race, replace that race's PNGs and keep the rule. Copper-legs pins a texture per part inside its `.jem` instead of using blank overlays. The elf only adds ears, so vanilla overlays work.

**Animations (in the `.jem`)**
- Copper wheel (`villager5.jem`): the wheel submodel is animated from the `body` top-level entry with `wheel.rx = limb_swing * 0.8`. Animations must be on a parent bone, not inside a submodel. Confirmed correct in the user's file.
- Kobold tail (`villager7.jem`): chain `tail` > `bone` > `bone2` under `body`, animated from the `body` entry:
  ```
  "animations": [ { "tail.ry": "torad(18) * sin(limb_swing * 38) * limb_speed",
                    "bone.ry": "torad(14) * sin(limb_swing * 38 - 45) * limb_speed",
                    "bone2.ry": "torad(14) * sin(limb_swing * 38 - 90) * limb_speed" } ]
  ```
  This is **not working** (see issues).

**Build tooling** (`build_rp.py`, `build_datapack.py`): `build_rp.py <extracted original pack dir>` copies the original pack, rewrites all `.properties`, generates blank overlays, copies the working `villager5.jem` and `villager7.jem` from the current directory, and zips the result. Note the script overwrites the pack's `.jem` files with whatever is next to it, so keep those up to date with the Blockbench exports.

### Build pipeline (2026-11-10, replaces build_rp.py, which is stale)
Run in this order after changing art or Blockbench exports (all are re-runnable; jar defaults to `%APPDATA%\.minecraft\versions\26.3\26.3.jar`):
1. `python normalize_jem.py <cem>/villager2.jem ... villager8.jem`: vanilla body pivots, race neck pivots plus `head.ty`, kobold tail animation, texture pins routed through `villager.png` (part-level pins removed).
2. `python build_dwarf.py`: dwarf model, skin, remapped vanilla overlays.
3. `python build_variants.py`: random looks. Hobbit, elf and dwarf get 4 hair colors (eye colors for the bald elf) x 3 skin tones x accessory on/off. Light hair (blonde, grey) is skipped on the deep skin tone (`LIGHT_HAIR`/`DARK_SKIN`), so hobbit has 22, dwarf 20 and elf 24. Fishfolk schemes recolor only the green skin band (`FISH_SKIN`, 75-165 deg); the blue pants keep their color. Copper legs and wheel get vanilla, exposed, weathered and oxidized. Fishfolk and kobold get 4 color schemes x accessory. Variants are numbered `N01..`; `random/.../villager.properties` is regenerated from scratch. This step also clears the copper base mini-block (unemployed constructs hold nothing).
3b. `python build_babies.py`: baby models (`cem/villager_baby*.jem`; vanilla 26.3 baby geometry read from `BabyVillagerModel`, with EMF part names head/headwear/headwear2/nose/body/bodywear/arms/legs), baby skins and the same variant lists as the adults, plus transparent baby biome overlays for copper, fishfolk and kobold. Hobbit, elf and dwarf babies wear the vanilla baby biome overlays.
4. `python build_race_professions.py`: copper (product mini-block plus tool), fishfolk (sea-themed) and kobold (underground-themed) profession overlays, plus transparent biome/level overlays for copper legs (copper wheel, fishfolk and kobold already had them).
5. `python build_contract_textures.py` and `python build_datapack.py` for contracts and the datapack.
Copper weathering (datapack + pack): the stage (0 copper, 1 exposed, 2 weathered, 3 oxidized) is the `races.copper` score, shown through per-stage teams `race_copper_<legs|wheel>[_exposed|_weathered|_oxidized]`. Base skins have one rule per stage team; model and overlay rules list all four (`race_teams.py` defines the names and the generators expand them). A minute tick ages unwaxed constructs (1/60 chance, so about an hour per stage). Honeycomb waxes; an axe removes the wax or scrapes off one stage. Ops can force a stage with `races:copper/set_<stage>`. The random copper variants were replaced by this.
Race voices (added 2026-11-10): `python build_sounds.py` writes Entity Sound Features rules under `assets/minecraft/esf/entity/villager/` for ambient, yes, no, trade, hurt, death and celebrate. Each `<event>.properties` matches race teams (copper by weathering stage: regular, weathered, oxidized golem sounds) and points at `<event><k>.json` sound lists. These reuse vanilla .ogg files with new pitch and volume, and the script checks every file exists in the game version. The last rule (`sounds.N=1`) keeps vanilla for everyone else. Client mods: ETF + EMF + ESF; players without ESF hear normal villagers. ESF's changelog confirms 26.1/26.2 (0.8.2, needs ETF 7.2+/EMF 3.3+); 26.3 support and `teams` conditions in ESF are not yet confirmed in game. Custom trades for non-hobbit races are on hold by decision.
Preview anything offline: `python preview_jem.py <model.jem> out.png <base.png> [overlay.png ...] [--yaw 30]`.
Hobbit snow/taiga overlays: boots removed (feet boxes and lower leg rows cleared) on 2026-11-10.

## 5. Known issues (current)

### A. Kobold tail does not animate (FIX APPLIED 2026-11-10, awaiting in-game check)
Resolution: binding was fine (the wheel uses the same body-entry pattern and works). The expressions were wrong: `* limb_speed` made the tail dead while standing, `limb_swing * 38` was ~57x leg frequency, phase offsets were degrees inside a radian `sin()`, and `ry` on a tail tilted 70 deg mostly spins it about its own length. New: age-driven sway (`sin(age * 0.12)`, about 2.6 s period), wider while walking, `tail.rz` for the root and `ry` for `bone`/`bone2`, 0.8 rad lag per segment. `normalize_jem.py` (ANIMATIONS table) rewrites these channels every run, so Blockbench exports cannot lose or revert them. If it still does not move, run the constant test below (`"tail.rz": "torad(45)"`). The original analysis follows.

State: the animation block above was re-inserted into the `body` entry of the user's `villager7.jem` (the ids match his tail chain), but the user reports the tail still does not sway.

Hypotheses, in rough order of likelihood:
1. **The animation block gets lost on each Blockbench export.** Blockbench's CEM exporter does not keep hand-written `animations`. The user re-exports often, and an earlier export already dropped it once. Check the file actually shipped in the pack still contains the block. Fix: a post-export patch step in `build_rp.py` that injects the animations into the `body` entry every build, instead of hand-editing.
2. **Wrong trig units.** If EMF's `sin()` takes radians (as in OptiFine), `sin(limb_swing * 38)` oscillates about 38 times per unit of walk distance and looks like a blur or nothing. Try the vanilla-style form: `sin(limb_swing * 0.6662) * limb_speed * <amplitude>` and test rotation units (radians via `torad()` vs raw). Verify against EMF's documented variable/func list for the installed version.
3. **Bone name collision or lookup.** `bone` and `bone2` are generic and may be ambiguous. Rename to `kobold_tail1`, `kobold_tail2`, `kobold_tail3` (and update the animation keys), and check the EMF/ETF log for animation parse errors (enable EMF debug/logging and read `latest.log`).
4. **Axis.** The tail may need `rz` (or `rx`) rather than `ry` depending on how it was modeled in Blockbench. Test each axis with a large constant value first to prove the binding works at all, for example `"tail.ry": "torad(45)"`.
5. EMF may not match the `models.N` + `teams.N` rule for the animated variant the same way, so confirm which `.jem` is actually being rendered (change a visible detail in the file as a canary).

Suggested debug path: set a constant rotation on `tail` first. If it does not move, the problem is binding or export (1, 3, 5). If it moves, the problem is the expression (2, 4).

### B. Kobold upper body sits too high (FIX APPLIED 2026-11-10, awaiting in-game check)
Resolution: hypothesis confirmed. Every other villager*.jem uses vanilla pivots; only the kobold did not, and the predicted offsets reproduce the symptom (bodywear drawn at y 20-34 instead of 4-18). `normalize_jem.py` rewrites only the top-level `translate` of each named part to vanilla, which moves pivots without moving geometry (boxes and first-level child translates are absolute). Applied to `villager7.jem`; `build_rp.py` now runs it on villager2-7 every build. Follow-up: static `rotate` values (arms 43, headwear2 90) DO apply in game even with a `"this.rx": 0` animation, so the first version pushed the arms ~3.7 px forward. The normalizer now shifts coords of rotated parts by (I - R^-1)(pivot delta); verified zero geometry change on every part. Re-run `python normalize_jem.py <file>.jem` after any Blockbench export. The original analysis follows.

Symptom: the body and all sub-parts render noticeably higher than in Blockbench. The body's lower edge sits roughly level with the head's lower edge.

Working hypothesis: the engine uses **vanilla pivots** for the named parts (head/headwear/headwear2/body/bodywear at y=24, nose 26, arms 21.05, legs 12, with legs at x=+-2) but draws boxes relative to the `.jem` pivot. The offset then equals (vanilla - kobold) per part: head +5, body +16, arms +5, legs +4. The hobbit model (`villager2.jem`) keeps all vanilla pivots and renders correctly, which supports this.

Current kobold pivot values (the `translate` of each top-level part) versus vanilla (checked 2026-10-24):

| part | kobold | vanilla |
|---|---|---|
| head, headwear, headwear2 | y -19 | -24 |
| body | y -24 (user already changed) | -24 |
| bodywear | y -8 | -24 |
| nose | -21 (z 3) | -26 |
| arms | -16.05 (z 0.05) | -21.05 (z 1.05) |
| right_leg / left_leg | -8, x -1 / +1 | -12, x -2 / +2 |

The audit at that time reported only head, bodywear, headwear and headwear2 as still off (y), but the nose, arms and legs were not yet at vanilla values either when I first dumped them. **Re-run the comparison on the current file.**

Fix options:
- In Blockbench, move each named group's origin to the vanilla value (as the hobbit model does) and re-export. Adjust the child cubes so the model looks identical.
- Or write a normalizer script (build step) that sets the vanilla translate for each named part and compensates cube coordinates and child translates by the delta. Conventions: top-level `translate` = -(Blockbench origin); top-level box coordinates are absolute (y up); children encode (-parent translate + relative offset). Verify these on villager2.jem before trusting them.
- Changing pivots affects animations: rotations pivot around the origin. Vanilla pivots are what the stock walk/head animations expect, so matching vanilla should make them behave better, not worse.

Diagnostic: diff `villager7.jem` against `villager2.jem` part by part and check whether the observed in-game offsets match the table of deltas exactly. If they do, the hypothesis is confirmed.

### B2. Heads of short races disconnect when turning/shaking (FIX APPLIED 2026-11-10, awaiting in-game check)
The game turns, nods and shakes the head around the vanilla neck (y=24). Hobbit (neck 20), kobold (19) and dwarf (16) heads sit lower, so they swung around a point above their neck. Elf, copper and fishfolk heads start at 24 and were fine. Fix in `normalize_jem.py` (`NECK` table): the head group (head, nose, headwear, headwear2) keeps pivots at the race's neck, and a `"head.ty": "head.ty + <drop>"` animation moves the game's head pivot down to it each frame. Verified offline with `preview_jem.py` head poses. **In-game check:** if a fixed head now sits too high or low at rest by twice its drop, flip `TY_SIGN` and re-run the normalizer.

### C. Smaller open items
- **EMF teams support** for `models.N` is unconfirmed in documentation (works in practice for display).
- **Essence items:** the original pack's `items/glass_bottle.json` references essence custom_model_data models, but `textures/item` contains no PNGs (only `desktop.ini`) and a `copper_essence` model is missing. Not related to races, but it will log errors.
- `pack.mcmeta` is legacy-format (`pack_format 42` with `supported_formats` 4..999999). It works; modernizing is optional.
- The audit script flags `textures/entity/villager/villager.png` as missing. That is a false positive (vanilla texture).
- Deleting `desktop.ini` and stray files from the pack is worth doing before release.
